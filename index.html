GuitarSQUAT

The rhythm game you play with your legs.

  1. Put your laptop on a table and stand 2–3 m back so the camera sees your whole body (head to feet).
  2. Notes scroll toward the ring. Squat so you hit the bottom as a note reaches the ring.
  3. Purple bars are holds: stay down until the bar ends.
  4. Pass both songs (60%+ accuracy) to beat the game.
